What we'll cover

Five money leaks

Most roofing offices have at least one of these. I'll show where each one happens on a real claim file, with the homeowner's details removed, and the fix you can put in place the next morning.

  1. 01

    Depreciation that never gets claimed

    On many replacement cost policies, the carrier holds back depreciation until the work is done and the paperwork is in. If nobody owns that step, the money sits.

  2. 02

    Checks stuck at the mortgage company

    When the lender is on the check, its loss draft department has to sign off. Without the right paperwork and a follow-up date, checks sit for months.

  3. 03

    Items that never got supplemented

    Code items and damage found at tear-off get missed. A clean supplement is documentation: photos, measurements, the code or reason, and a clear line-item request.

  4. 04

    Paperwork that stalls the final payment

    Final invoice, completion photos, certificate of completion. What carriers and lenders usually ask for, and how to send it once, complete.

  5. 05

    Receivables nobody's chasing

    Deductibles, supplement payments, and final checks that are owed but untracked. A simple weekly routine that keeps money moving.
