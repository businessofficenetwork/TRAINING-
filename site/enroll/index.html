What you'll learn

Six weeks, one claim file

You'll work the same claim file all course, from the day it comes in to the day the last check clears. By week 5 you've taken one job from intake to final payment.

  1. 01
    Nov 3

    Claim intake and the job file

    Open a claim file that holds everything the rest of the job will need, and spot what's missing.

  2. 02
    Nov 10

    Reading the carrier estimate

    RCV, ACV, depreciation, and the deductible: know what's been paid, what's held back, and why.

  3. 03
    Nov 17

    Supplements as documentation

    Find what the estimate missed and document it with photos, measurements, and code so it can be approved.

  4. 04
    Dec 1

    Adjuster communication and follow-up

    Submit, follow up on a schedule, and answer a partial denial with documents instead of arguments.

  5. 05
    Dec 8

    Getting paid

    Release recoverable depreciation, help homeowners get mortgage checks released, and collect the deductible properly.

  6. 06
    Dec 15

    AR, collections, and handoff to production

    Run a weekly money routine for the whole office. Then work a second file start to finish on your own.

What this course is, and isn't

It is

Training on documentation: photos, measurements, code citations, clean files, and steady follow-up. The contractor documents its own scope of work.

It isn't

Public adjusting. Contractors don't negotiate the claim on the homeowner's behalf, and rules on what contractors may say to carriers vary by state. You'll learn what to check in yours.
